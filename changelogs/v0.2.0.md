# Release Notes: v0.2.0

**Release Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Tag:** `v0.2.0`  
**Status:** Released / Phase 2: Teams, Categories & Items Setup

---

## 🚀 Overview

`v0.2.0` delivers the complete competition structural foundation for **MeeladFest**. This release allows festival administrators to configure competition teams, age-range categories, and festival items with customized points scoring and team participation limits.

---

## 📋 Highlights & Changes

### 🛡️ Teams Management
- **API Routes**: `GET`, `POST` on `/api/fests/[festId]/teams`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` on `/api/fests/[festId]/teams/[teamId]`.
- **Validation**: Enforces unique team names and codes per festival.
- **Admin UI**: `/dashboard/fests/[festId]/teams` table view with inline search, add/edit modals, and color code tags.

### 🏷️ Categories Management (Age-Group Division)
- **API Routes**: `GET`, `POST` on `/api/fests/[festId]/categories`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` on `/api/fests/[festId]/categories/[categoryId]`.
- **Validation**: Validates age boundaries (`minAge`, `maxAge`) to ensure non-overlapping categories.
- **Admin UI**: `/dashboard/fests/[festId]/categories` management dashboard with category creation and editing.

### 🏆 Competition Items Setup
- **API Routes**: `GET`, `POST` on `/api/fests/[festId]/items`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` on `/api/fests/[festId]/items/[itemId]`.
- **Item Types**: Supports both `single` and `group` item types.
- **Custom Points & Limits**: Allows configuring custom points for 1st, 2nd, 3rd positions and enforcing `maxParticipantsPerTeam` limits for single events.
- **Admin UI**: `/dashboard/fests/[festId]/items` with category filtering and item configuration.

---

## 🔒 Access Control & Safety
- All API routes protected using `getFestPermission(festId, userId)` ensuring only festival owners or sub-admins with permission can modify structure.
- Prevents deletion of categories or teams if linked items or participants exist.
