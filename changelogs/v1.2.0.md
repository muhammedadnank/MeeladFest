# Changelog v1.2.0 - Islamic Modern UI/UX Redesign & Design Tokens

**Release Date:** July 29, 2026

## 🚀 Overview

Version 1.2.0 delivers a ground-up visual and architectural UI/UX redesign of the entire MeeladFest platform. Adopting a bespoke **Islamic Modern Aesthetic**, the release incorporates custom design tokens (Emerald, Gold Accent, Warm Cream background, Amiri typography), reusable UI primitives, redesigned public portal pages, modernized authentication cards, and streamlined admin dashboard navigation.

---

## 🎨 Key Features & UI/UX Upgrades

### 1. 🌿 Design Tokens & Theme Foundation
- **Bespoke Color Palette**: Integrated `#0f3d26` (Deep Emerald), `#1a5c3a` (Medium Emerald), `#c8962a` (Warm Gold), `#faf7f0` (Warm Cream Background), and `#e6decb` (Warm Border).
- **Typography Integration**: Paired traditional Google Font **Amiri** (for headers and Islamic branding) with modern sans-serif **Inter** (for body text and tabular data).
- **UI Primitives Library (`src/components/ui/`)**:
  - `GeometricPattern`: High-fidelity SVG Islamic geometric pattern overlay component.
  - `LiveBadge`: Animated real-time pulse badge for active festival indicators.
  - `SectionTitle`: Styled section headers with gold dot accents and subheadings.
  - `LoadingSkeleton`: Polished skeleton loaders matching cream & emerald card layouts.
  - `EmptyState`: Contextual empty state illustrations for missing data.

### 2. 🏛️ Public Homepage Redesign (`/`)
- **Hero Section**: Arabic typography banner ("مَهْرَجَانُ المِيلَادِ"), headline, subtext, and dual call-to-action buttons (*Explore Festivals* and *Live Leaderboards*).
- **Live Stats Bar**: 3-column emerald bar showing total active festivals, participating teams, and total registered items.
- **Fest Cards**: Rich festival cards featuring gold badge indicators, date/venue metadata, and direct access links.
- **Feature Highlights Grid**: 2x2 grid highlighting real-time standings, individual championship scoring, instantly searchable digital certificates, and multi-subadmin management.

### 3. 🏆 Public Festival Portal (`/fests/[slug]`)
- **Header & Navigation**: Sticky tab bar for seamless switching between Team Standings, Individual Championship, Timeline, Photo Gallery, and FAQs.
- **Top 3 Winner Podiums**: Custom podium layouts for 1st (Gold 🥇), 2nd (Silver 🥈), and 3rd (Bronze 🥉) rank holders with score breakdowns and rank badges.
- **Category Filtering**: Category filter pills (Sub-Junior, Junior, Senior, etc.) enabling quick rank lookups.
- **Certificate Lookup Engine**: Instant chest number lookup modal with immediate preview and download options.

### 4. 🔒 Auth & Admin Dashboard Redesign
- **Authentication Cards (`/login`, `/register`)**: Redesigned auth screens with warm cream backdrops, gold border highlights, custom role toggles (Super Admin vs Sub-Admin), and emerald action buttons.
- **Main Admin Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: Clean fest management hub displaying overall platform statistics, active festival cards, and quick festival creation triggers.
- **Festival Admin Hub (`/dashboard/fests/[festId]`)**: Redesigned navigation bar (`<FestAdminNav />`), status metrics (Teams, Items, Participants, Results), and quick action shortcuts.

---

## 🛠️ Codebase & Performance Enhancements

1. **Hydration Parity**: Ensured 100% SSR-to-client hydration alignment across countdown timers and dynamic tabs.
2. **Tailwind Config Extensions**: Extended `tailwind.config.ts` with custom colors (`cream`, `emerald-950`, `emerald-800`, `gold-500`, `border-warm`), custom fonts, border radii (`rounded-card`), and keyframe animations.
3. **Responsive Optimization**: Mobile-first design testing ensuring smooth layouts across screen sizes down to 375px.
