# Release Notes: v0.3.0

**Release Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Tag:** `v0.3.0`  
**Status:** Released / Phase 4: Participants & Group Entries Data Entry

---

## 🚀 Overview

`v0.3.0` introduces comprehensive participant registration and team group entry management for **MeeladFest**. This release enables festival administrators to register individual participants with unique chest numbers and enroll team group entries into group events.

---

## 📋 Highlights & Changes

### 👤 Individual Participants Management
- **API Routes**: `GET`, `POST` on `/api/fests/[festId]/participants`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` on `/api/fests/[festId]/participants/[participantId]`.
- **Unique Chest Numbers**: Enforces chest number uniqueness across each festival.
- **Participation Validation**: Validates category eligibility and item participation limits (`maxParticipantsPerTeam`).
- **CSV Data Export**: Supports exporting participant rosters to CSV format directly from the dashboard.

### 👥 Team Group Entries
- **API Routes**: `GET`, `POST` on `/api/fests/[festId]/group-entries`, `PATCH`, `DELETE` on `/api/fests/[festId]/group-entries/[groupEntryId]`.
- **Validation**: Enforces group item type compatibility and prevents duplicate team entries per item.
- **Chest Number Mapping**: Captures member chest numbers for self-service certificate verification.

### 🖥️ Dashboard UI
- **Admin Management Page**: `/dashboard/fests/[festId]/participants` providing unified tabbed management for individual participants and team group entries.
- **Filters & Search**: Instant searching by chest number, name, or phone number, with team and category drop-down filters.

---

## 🔒 Permission & Safety Safeguards
- Gated by `participants` permission toggle or fest ownership.
- Safe deletion check preventing removal of participants with recorded competition results.
