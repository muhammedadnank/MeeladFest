# MeeladFest Release v1.4.0: Islamic Cultural Aesthetics & Visual Overhaul

**Release Date:** July 29, 2026  
**Theme:** Full Islamic Cultural Aesthetics, Girih Patterns, Arabic Calligraphy & Malayalam Localization  

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Major Changes

### 1. 🕌 Header & Footer Cultural Branding
- **Navbar Bismillah Banner**: Added top calligraphy banner featuring `بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ` and localized Malayalam portal tagline.
- **Salawat Footer Banner**: Integrated central Salawat banner (`مَوْلِدُ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ`) and `Girih` lattice background overlays.

### 2. 👑 Hero Section & Statistics Bar Overhaul
- **Islamic Mihrab Arch Theme**: Refined Hero section with dual-language Prophet Salawat Calligraphy (`اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ...`).
- **Bilingual Stats Display**: Combined Arabic numerals (`١+`, `١٠٠+`, `١٠٠٪`) with localized Malayalam statistics labels.

### 3. ⏱️ Festival Countdown & Schedule Banner
- **Girih Geometric Lattice Overlays**: Embedded signature Islamic SVG star/lattice patterns.
- **Dual-Language Timers**: Added Malayalam & Arabic countdown unit labels (`ദിവസം / أيام`, `മണിക്കൂർ / ساعات`, etc.).

### 4. 🎨 Feature Grid & Fest Cards Aesthetics
- **Gold-Emerald Palette**: Standardized royal emerald (`emerald-950/900`) backgrounds with gold (`gold-500/200`) accents.
- **Calligraphic Titles**: Sub-titled all festival management features with classic Arabic calligraphy.

### 5. ۞ UI Components & Dividers
- **Islamic Crescent Motif (`☽`) & Rub el Hizb (`۞`)**: Enhanced EmptyState component and section dividers with authentic cultural iconography.
